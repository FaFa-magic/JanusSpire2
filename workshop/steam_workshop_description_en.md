# AZUR LANE: JANUS CHARACTER MOD

Bring Janus, a J-class destroyer from *Azur Lane*, into the Spire. She must overcome her timidity while recording experiences, treasuring memories, and drawing strength from her unique **Diary** card pile.

This mod requires **STS2-RitsuLib 0.6.2 or later**.

![Mod Content](images/janus_workshop_content.png)

## MOD CONTENT

- 1 new playable character: **Janus**
- **89 + 14** new cards: 89 standard and multiplayer cards, plus 14 Ancient, generated, and Status cards
- **20** new relics
- **3** new potions
- 1 new Ancient: **Queen Elizabeth**
- 1 new event: **A Kitten's Card Game**
- **7** selectable outfits, each with its own combat, merchant, and rest-site Spine model

## DIARY MECHANICS

### DIARY

The Diary is Janus's unique card area. Cards and keywords can place cards into it, activate effects from it, or Exhaust its contents as a resource. Recording, organizing, and recalling cards from the Diary form the heart of Janus's deck-building strategy.

### RECORD & TRANSCRIBE

**Record**: After this card is played, if it is not Exhausted, add it to your Diary.

**Transcribe**: After this card is played, add a copy of it to your Diary. Its following effect is active while the card is in your Diary.

### RECOLLECTION

While a card with **Recollection** is in your Diary, meeting its listed condition causes its projection to appear automatically in the Extra Hand. It can then be played from the Diary. The condition resets after it is played.

### RALLY

If you do not have enough Energy to play a card with **Rally**, you may Exhaust cards from your Diary equal to the missing Energy to play it.

### KEEPSAKE & STICKER

A card with **Keepsake** is added to your Diary at the start of combat.

**Sticker** cards can be Upgraded any number of times. Whenever the Exhaust Pile contains 4 Sticker cards with the same name and Upgrade level, remove them and add an Upgraded copy of that Sticker card to your Diary.

### INSPIRATION

While a card with **Inspiration X** is not in your Hand, right-click it and choose to Exhaust X cards from your Diary to add that card to your Hand.

## EXTRA FEATURES

- Switch freely between **7 Janus outfits** in the mod settings.
- Choose between two custom Janus card-frame styles.
- Multiplayer pointing and rock-paper-scissors visuals can use either the hand models or the leg models.
- The Queen Elizabeth Ancient and A Kitten's Card Game event can be enabled or disabled independently.
- Includes a custom Diary interface, Extra Hand interactions, character card art, power icons, relics, potions, a rest-site diary option, and themed replacements for selected base-game assets.
